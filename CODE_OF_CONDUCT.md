# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct establishes the standards of behavior expected from all participants in this project, including contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community members.

The objective is to maintain a respectful, constructive, and inclusive environment that supports collaboration and the exchange of technical knowledge.

## 2. Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect, professionalism, and courtesy.
- Communicate constructively and provide criticism focused on technical matters.
- Welcome reasonable questions, suggestions, and differing viewpoints.
- Accept constructive feedback and engage in good-faith discussions.
- Respect the contributions, time, and expertise of other participants.
- Follow the project's contribution guidelines and applicable community standards.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior

The following conduct is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or derogatory language directed at individuals or groups.
- Deliberate disruption of technical discussions or collaborative work.
- Publishing private or confidential information without authorization.
- Repeatedly engaging in abusive, hostile, or bad-faith behavior.

Disagreements about technical decisions are acceptable when expressed respectfully and supported by reasoned arguments.

## 4. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project-related activities, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, code reviews, and other communication channels used for project collaboration.

## 5. Reporting Inappropriate Behavior

Instances of unacceptable behavior should be reported privately to the project maintainers through an appropriate communication channel.

Reports should include sufficient information to allow the maintainers to assess the situation. All reports will be handled with appropriate discretion, subject to the information necessary to investigate the matter.

## 6. Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the nature and severity of a violation, corrective measures may include a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project activities.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the circumstances and aimed at preserving a safe and constructive environment.

## 7. Acknowledgment

Participation in this project implies a commitment to respectful communication and responsible collaboration.

By contributing to this project, participants agree to follow these principles and help maintain a professional and welcoming community.
